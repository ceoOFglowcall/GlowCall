# GlowCall Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-05

GlowCall values your privacy. This policy explains what data is handled by the app.

## 1. Data We Collect

GlowCall stores basic in-app preferences on device (for example language and selected preset).  
GlowCall does not require account creation for core app usage.

## 2. Payments

If you purchase a subscription, payment processing is handled by Apple through App Store infrastructure.  
GlowCall does not directly collect your payment card details.

## 3. Analytics and Tracking

GlowCall does not use third-party advertising SDKs in this version.

## 4. Data Sharing

GlowCall does not sell your personal data.

## 5. Your Choices

You can remove app data by deleting the app from your device.
